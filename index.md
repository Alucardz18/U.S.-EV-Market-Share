# U.S.-EV-Market-Share

This project analyzes the growth and distribution of electric vehicles across the United States using state-level vehicle registration data. The goal is to identify where EV adoption is accelerating, how EVs compare to traditional fuel types, and what insights policymakers and automakers can use to guide infrastructure planning.
